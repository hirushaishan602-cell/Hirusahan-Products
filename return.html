Return Policy

At Hirusahan Products, we ensure the highest quality for our spices. Our return policy is as follows:

  • Returns are accepted only for damaged or incorrect items received.
  • Since these are food products (spices), we cannot accept returns for items once the original packaging/seal is opened.
  • Customers must notify us of any issues within 7 days of delivery.
  • To initiate a return, please contact us at +94 72 396 1127 with your order details.
